What we can help with

Choosing between products

Tell us what you have already tried and what you are hoping to change. We will tell you which of our products is the closest fit, and we will tell you when the answer is "none of them" — that happens more often than you would expect from a company that makes money selling supplements.

Certificates of analysis

Send the lot number printed on your bottle and we will send back the independent lab report for that lot. No account, no form, no marketing sequence attached.

Returns

Sixty days, opened or unopened. Email support@moddose.com with your order number and we will send a return label. You do not have to explain yourself, though we will ask what did not work if you feel like telling us. Full detail on the shipping and returns page.

Subscriptions

You can skip, reschedule or cancel from your account, and from the link in every pre-shipment email. If any of that ever fails to work, email us and we will cancel it manually the same day.

Wholesale and stockists

We work with a small number of clinics and gyms. Email hello@moddose.com with your business details and expected volumes.

Medical questions

We cannot give medical advice, and we will not pretend otherwise. If your question is about an interaction with a prescription medication, a health condition, pregnancy or a dose for a child, the right person to ask is a pharmacist or your doctor. We are happy to send you the full ingredient list to take with you.
