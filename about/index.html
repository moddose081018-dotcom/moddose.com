Our standards

What we will and won't do

A supplement company is mostly a set of decisions about what to leave out. Here are ours, written down so you can hold us to them.

Why this exists

The cognitive supplement shelf is unusually bad. Doses are hidden inside proprietary blends, prices swing forty percent depending on which discount code you happened to find, and the marketing borrows the language of medicine without any of the obligations that come with it.

None of that is necessary. You can run a supplement business where the amounts are printed, the price is the price, and the marketing copy would survive being read out loud by a regulator. That is the whole idea. It is not a very exciting founding story, but it is an accurate one.

Dosing

Every active ingredient is listed with the amount per serving. Where a formula uses a blend, the blend's components are broken out individually. We do not use proprietary blends to conceal that an ingredient is present in trace amounts, because that is the only thing proprietary blends reliably conceal.

Where a human research literature exists for an ingredient, we dose to the amounts used in that literature or we leave the ingredient out. A sub-threshold dose is a marketing ingredient, not an active one.

Testing

Lots are tested by an accredited independent laboratory for identity, potency and contaminants before release. A certificate of analysis for the specific lot you received is available on request — email support@moddose.com with the lot number printed on your bottle.

Ask any supplement company for a lot-specific certificate of analysis. The ones that send it within a day are a different kind of business from the ones that send you a marketing PDF, and the difference is worth knowing before you spend money.

Pricing

Our prices sit on a fixed, published ladder of price points. Two consequences follow, and we think both are good ones.

  • There is no permanent sale. We do not print an inflated original price and cross it out, because a discount against a price nobody ever paid is not a discount.
  • Everyone pays the same. There is no code circulating in a forum that gets a stranger a better deal than you got.

The one real discount we offer is Subscribe & Save at 20%, which is ongoing rather than a first-order hook, and which you can leave with one click.

What we will not claim

We sell dietary supplements. They are not medicines, and we will not describe them as though they were. Specifically, we will not say or imply that anything we sell:

  • treats, prevents, cures or diagnoses any condition, including attention, sleep, mood or memory conditions;
  • is equivalent to, a substitute for, or an alternative to any prescription medication;
  • is "clinically proven" — individual ingredients may have research behind them, which is a much narrower statement and the one we will actually make.

If you find copy anywhere on this site that crosses those lines, tell us and we will fix it. That is a genuine request, not a rhetorical one.

Who should not buy from us

People under 18. People who are pregnant or breastfeeding. Anyone looking for a substitute for treatment they have been prescribed. Anyone hoping a supplement will fix a problem that is really about sleep, workload or health — it will not, and we would rather say so on this page than take the order.

Talk to us

Questions about sourcing, testing or a specific ingredient go to hello@moddose.com. We answer ingredient questions properly, including the ones where the honest answer is that the evidence is thin.
