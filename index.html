How we operate

01

The amount is on the label

Every active ingredient is listed with its exact amount. If a formula uses a blend, the blend's components are broken out. A supplement that hides its doses is hiding something about its doses.

Questions

Before you buy

Do these actually work?

Honestly: it depends on the person, the formula and what you are measuring. Response to this category varies a lot between individuals, and plenty of people notice nothing. That is exactly why we sell a 15-serving trial size and take returns for 60 days on opened bottles. Run the experiment cheaply before you spend real money.

Are these a substitute for prescription medication?

No. These are dietary supplements, not medicines, and nothing we sell is intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition — including attention, sleep or mood conditions. If you are considering supplements as an alternative to prescribed treatment, please talk to your prescriber first.

How does your pricing work?

Every product sits on a fixed, published set of price points. We do not run permanent sales, and we do not display a crossed-out price we never charged. Subscribers pay 20% less, which is a real, ongoing discount rather than a first-order trick.

What does third-party tested mean here?

Lots are sent to an accredited independent laboratory for identity, potency and contaminant testing before release. A certificate of analysis for the lot you received is available by email on request — ask for it, and check that the numbers match the label.

How fast do you ship?

Orders placed before 2pm on a business day are dispatched the same day, otherwise the next business day. Shipping is free over $50 and a flat $5.95 below that. Full detail is on the shipping and returns page.

Can I return something I have opened?

Yes. Sixty days, opened or unopened. If a formula did nothing for you, that is a legitimate reason for a refund and you do not need to argue it with anyone.

New formulas, new lot results

Occasional email. New products, restocks, and the certificate of analysis when a new lot lands. Nothing else.

No spam, and we never sell your address.
