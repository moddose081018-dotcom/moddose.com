How to take it

Take 7 capsules in the morning on an empty stomach, or split across breakfast and mid-morning. Many people run five days on, two days off.

Who it's for: Experienced buyers who have already worked out that they respond, and want coverage rather than clarity.

Questions

Seven capsules is a lot. Can I take fewer?

You can take a partial serving, but then you are taking a partial dose of everything, including the ingredients that were the reason you bought it.

Should I cycle it?

Five days on, two off is a common pattern with formulas this broad. It is convention rather than a documented requirement.
