How to take it

Mix one sachet into 300–400 ml of cold water. Use on demanding days only. Avoid within six hours of bedtime.

Who it's for: People with occasional high-demand days who do not want a daily stimulant habit.

Can I take this every day?

We do not recommend it. Stimulant tolerance builds quickly, and the value of this formula depends on it staying occasional.
