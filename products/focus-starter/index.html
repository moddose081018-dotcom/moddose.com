Questions

How long before I notice anything?

Effects vary between individuals, and some people notice nothing at all. Give it the full fifteen servings before deciding, and pay attention to sleep and caffeine as confounders.

Can I take it with coffee?

Many people do, but stack stimulants carefully and start with less caffeine than usual on your first few days.
